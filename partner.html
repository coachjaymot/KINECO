Partnerships

Let's Build Something Together.

KINECO works with employers, community organizations, parks departments, city agencies, and private groups to bring purposeful movement programming to the people who need it most.

Why partner with KINECO?

We bring the programming, the expertise, and the community relationships. You bring the space, the people, or the resources. Together we create something neither of us could build alone.

Flexible partnership models From single events to long-term contracts — built around what works for you.
